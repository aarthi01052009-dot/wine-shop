Our story

Named after a river that never stopped moving.

AARU — the Tamil word for river — is what we called this shop because that's what we wanted it to feel like: something that keeps moving, keeps finding new bottles, and never sits still with the same old stock.

We started AARU because Salem deserved a wine shop that treated its customers like regulars, not footfall. Most shelves in town are stacked wide and explained by no one. We wanted the opposite: a smaller, better-chosen range, and people behind the counter who can tell you what a bottle actually tastes like — not just its price.

We're not the biggest shop in Salem. We just want to be the one you trust to pick right.

Every bottle on our shelf has been tried by someone on our team first. If we wouldn't take it home ourselves, it doesn't make the cut.

Why AARU is different

Three things we do differently

—

A curated shelf, not a crowded one

We stock fewer labels on purpose, so every bottle earns its place. Less time squinting at rows, more time leaving with something good.

—

Honest, upfront pricing

No pushy upselling, no confusing offers. What's on the shelf tag is what you pay, and we'll always tell you if something cheaper suits you better.

—

People who actually know the stock

Ask us what pairs with dinner or what's easy to start with — our team has tasted what's on the shelf and will give you a straight answer.
